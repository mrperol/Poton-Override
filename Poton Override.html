Poton Override

Run endlessly. Collect spray cans (+10) to refill the SPRAY bar, and stomp or spray enemies for combos. Grab a FAT CAP for auto-spray, a star for 5 seconds of invincibility and double shots, and hearts for extra lives. Fill the BUFF PAINT bar (30 cans), then press the green button to wipe out everything. Left stick: move, hold down to crouch (hold 0.3s on a platform to drop). Right buttons: jump, dash, spray, buff paint.
